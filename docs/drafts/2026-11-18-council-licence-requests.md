# Licence requests to councils (T-35): drafts for the owner to send

Agents do not contact outside parties; these are drafts for the owner. Each asks one plain question and says what
would be published. Send from your own address; record each reply in SOURCES.md.

## Auckland Council (zoning: 563,052 parcels, 23% of New Zealand)

To: the Auckland Council GIS / open data team (via aucklandcouncil.govt.nz contact form, or the address on the
"Unitary Plan Base Zone" item on ArcGIS Online).

> Subject: Use of the Unitary Plan Base Zone layer under CC BY 4.0
>
> Kia ora. I'm building Land Query, an open index of facts about land parcels in New Zealand (one row per LINZ parcel).
> I'd like to include, for each parcel in Auckland, the name of the Unitary Plan base zone that covers most of it,
> with Auckland Council credited as the source under CC BY 4.0.
>
> The layer's licence says it is CC BY 4.0, and also that "you are not permitted to copy or republish any substantial
> amount of the information ... without the prior written consent of Auckland Council". Could you confirm that
> publishing each parcel's zone name (not the zone polygons themselves), with attribution, is permitted under CC BY 4.0,
> or give that consent? The data would be refreshed monthly from your layer.
>
> Thank you.

## Wellington City Council (zoning, 2024 District Plan: 68,887 parcels)

To: District.Plan@wcc.govt.nz (named on the layer).

> Subject: Licence for the 2024 District Plan zones layer
>
> Kia ora. I'm building Land Query, an open index of facts about land parcels in New Zealand. I'd like to include, for
> each parcel in Wellington City, the 2024 District Plan zone that covers most of it, with Wellington City Council
> credited as the source. The "Zones - 2024 District Plan" layer says it was created for the District Plan and to
> contact you for other uses. Could it be used under CC BY 4.0 (as the council's older "WCC District Plan Zones" layer
> is), refreshed monthly?
>
> Thank you.

## Kāpiti Coast District Council and Greater Wellington Regional Council (the original T-35)

> Subject: Confirming CC BY 4.0 for your public map service layers
>
> Kia ora. I'm building Land Query, which compiles facts per land parcel from public sources, including layers from
> your public ArcGIS map services (zoning, overlays, flood and hazard layers). Your open data portal states CC BY 4.0,
> but some of these service layers are not listed in the portal's catalogue. Could you confirm that the portal-wide CC
> BY 4.0 statement covers the layers on your public map services, so we can publish values derived from them with
> attribution?
>
> Thank you.
