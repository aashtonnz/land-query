Land Query · parcel facts from public data
NZTM2000 · NZVD2016 · 9 Nov 2026

Every parcel in New Zealand, measured from public data.

Land Query compiles the facts about each land parcel from public sources: LINZ titles and survey, 1 m LiDAR, and council planning and asset layers. Each value records its source, licence and fetch time. The aim is to answer questions across a whole district, such as "which flat, sewered residential sections are big enough to subdivide?", in seconds rather than by checking parcels one at a time. Kāpiti Coast and Porirua are compiled in full with council data; any other parcel in the country gets a report on request from national LINZ data.

Nationwide

Any parcel in the country, in about a minute

Beyond the two councils, Land Query works from national LINZ data alone: parcels, titles, building outlines, addresses, the coastline, and the national 1 m LiDAR, whose surveys run from 2008 to 2026. It comes in two parts. A national index holds every parcel for area searches. A full report for one parcel is computed when someone asks for it.

What's in a parcel row

About a hundred facts per parcel

Each row is one LINZ primary parcel. Roads and water parcels are left out. Values are published, derived exactly from published data, or modelled, and each column says which. An empty value is never a measured 0. It means not mapped, not measured, or outside the source's coverage, as each column's notes say.

Title and survey

public
  • Legal description, area, parcel intent
  • Number of titles, easements, covenants
  • Share inside the district held

Terrain (1 m LiDAR)

public
  • Ground height (min, mean, max) and slope
  • Overland flow paths and upslope area
  • Whole-river catchment on a 10 m grid
  • Ground change 2021 → 2025 (Kāpiti)

public

How it's checked

Every number has to survive a fresh pair of eyes

The pipeline refuses to publish a run that is partial, built from a failed source, or that breaks a column's stated rule. Each finished stage is then reviewed by a reviewer who did not build it, working from the code and data alone. After a serious finding is fixed, the work is reviewed again, until a review finds nothing serious.
